[research] Simon Cockell

19 June 2026

Giorgio was right. interpreting results is much harder and longer than I anticipated. Raul originally assured me I'd have the help of others within the lab in this, since they have the relevant biological knowledge that I lack. but he afterwards started suggesting that I do a lot of reading on my own and make a stab at piecing together a story myself based on the results... which has left me feeling quite frazzled and incompetent and unqualified for this task.

I've been doing lots of reading, yes -- burrowing my way down several rabbit holes in the process. I'm not exactly sure what to focus on. from the trends I do observe in the data, it's all things that would be expected when comparing these different types of muscle diseases to FSHD. I'm not seeing anything novel that I feel would be a useful contribution to the literature or something to look more closely into. and I'm honestly feeling a bit like I've been hung out to dry without anyone to help me, since Raul told me to try this out on my own. I guess there's not much to do besides keep trying, then show him what I come up with and ask for his input.
