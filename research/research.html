research

overview: I conducted research this summer under Dr. Giorgio Tasca at the John Walton Muscular Dystrophy Research Centre (JWMDRC) in Newcastle-upon-Tyne, located in northeast England. I learned how to apply bioinformatics techniques to bulk RNA-sequencing data to compare the transcriptomic profile of facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD) to the transcriptomic profile of 9 other inflammatory myopathies and muscular dystrophies, including Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) and inclusion body myositis (IBM). this analysis technique unlocks insights into big biological data, revealing genetic targets for subsequent mechanistic investigation and/or clinical treatment.
