[research] Simon Cockell

19 June 2026

Simon Cockell. what a guy. this has been another week listening to the guy yap about bulk RNASeq analysis on 1.5-2x speed, with a daily side tangent reserved for whatever digital art his son drew that day.

I do feel like I'm becoming more comfortable with the methods and steps involved in bulk RNA sequencing analysis. as I learn more, I'm also realizing how ambiguous this is, in the sense that there are several ways to do any one thing, and different people have different opinions on what works better or worse. it's a lot less cut-and-dry than I initially thought (and hoped).

toward the end of this week, Raul sent me the files for the six external datasets and the internal data that I'll be working with this summer. I've started processing the files, aggregating the transcript-level counts to gene-level counts. many of these steps are very time-consuming, so I've been watching more Simon Cockell while I wait for steps to run.
