[research] week 1 thoughts

5 June 2026

how would you describe your experience adjusting to a new environment?

I think I'm doing decently well adjusting just by going with the flow. the people in the office here seem pretty social and welcoming, which has helped me integrate into the culture. people here take a full hour for lunch, even though they don't spend the whole time eating -- the rest of the time is for yapping.

my work this summer is purely dry lab (bioinformatics), so most of my time has been spent in the open office space at the Centre rather than in a benchside setting. it's surprisingly similar to the settings of my previous two summers at software engineering internships -- the same office vibe, and staring at a computer screen all day. I wasn't expecting it to be like this, but this is something I have experience with, which should help me in settling into this environment.

what differences have you noticed between this setting and UF?

the pace of work (at least in the basic research sector) seems a lot more moderate and laid-back than in my UF lab. the people are also generally more friendly, more likely to hang out outside of work, and more likely to invite you along. just today, I was invited out for Friday pub, a weekly tradition here at the Centre. I might've gone, too, if I hadn't had prior dinner plans with one of the other members of my lab.
