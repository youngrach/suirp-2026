[blog] food and fair

21 June 2026

this week I've been expanding my British food palette. on Thursday afternoon, I tagged along with Andrew from the office to Sainsbury's to pick up some biscuits for his office stash. we returned with custard creams, bourbon creams, Jaffa cakes, and chocolate HobNobs -- all British staples. custard and bourbon creams are (respectively) custard- and chocolate-flavored sandwich cookies. Jaffa cakes are flat, cakey cylinders with one side coated in chocolate and a layer of orange-flavored jam in the middle. Hobnobs are crispy oat biscuits with one side coated in chocolate. the Hobnobs were addictive. the creams were pretty good, but not on par with the Hobnobs. and I didn't like the Jaffa cakes at all.

Saturday morning, I walked to the French Oven, a bakery within Grainger Market, to try my first Cornish pasty (pronounced PAST-ee). it looked pretty much like a large, rotund empanada. and it tasted pretty much like one too, albeit the filling was a bit more wet. overall, not bad.

an amusement park has sprung up on a piece of empty grassland by the Town Moor this past week. it's The Hoppings, an annual summer "funfair" in Newcastle. it's free entry, so I wandered through it one day after dinner. there were some people on the rides, and more people in the food area. it got a bit overstimulating after awhile, so I wondered away to look at the cows. but it was really nice to see families and friends enjoying their evening at such a nostalgic, childhood-core occupation.
