[blog] final week thoughts

1 August 2026

I'm checking out of my housing accommodations today, after 2 months in this cozy half-underground room. it's been good to me. Newcastle has been good to me.

I went for one last run around the city today, hitting the Town Moor (my short run mainstay) with its many cows, Leazes Park, 3 bridges over the River Tyne, and the city centre. it was a wonderful way to say farewell to the sights and sounds that have grown so familiar this summer.

I'm not ready to leave -- not by a long shot. I could see myself living here. a big signal for me is the complete lack of homesickness I've experienced here (excluding the first day or two). when I studied abroad in Singapore 3 years ago, I was ready to return home by week 6. now I've spent 9 weeks here, and the more time passes, the stronger pull I feel to this place.

the city is so walkable. the green spaces are so lovely and so abundant (and so utilized). the people are so warm and friendly, in an unobtrusive way that sneaks up on you when you're not paying attention. there are numerous bakeries around town with strengths in different pastries, and they're all worth trying. the running routes, the cows (!!), the music, the pies -- I'll miss them all. the work culture is a breath of fresh air. British humor is my cup of tea (pun intended).

I feel a strong pull to this place. I feel like I belong here. maybe not for forever, but at least for now. is this real, or just an emotional byproduct of my leaving? I guess we'll see in a few months' time. either way, I refuse to believe this is my last time here. I will be back. I promise myself. I promise you, Newcastle.
