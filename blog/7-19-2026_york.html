[blog] York

19 July 2026

this Saturday, Andrew was headed to York for a football match, and he offered to show me around the town before the match. so off we went (via train).

it was an overcast day, and the streets were thronging with tourists. York itself is full of well-maintained old buildings, with a wall that can be walked upon encircling the old borders of the town. there was a colossal cathedral called the Minster, public gardens featuring the ruins of a walled structure, and bridges stretching over a river.

strolling through the streets, we got to walk through the street that supposedly inspired Diagon Alley in the Harry Potter movies. it was packed to the brim with people milling about, which added to the resemblance. later, as I did some solo exploring, I passed by so many gorgeous bakery window displays, a couple of street performances, and three separate couples getting married. it honestly fits; York is a beautiful place.

I felt like by existing in York, I was plugging into the place's rich history -- centuries of people walking its cobblestones, slaking their thirst in its pubs, and painstakingly building all the aging structures I saw around me. I was part of something much larger than myself. it was a pretty cool feeling.
