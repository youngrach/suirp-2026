[blog] Lewis Capaldi in the park

12 July 2026

this past Wednesday evening, Lewis Capaldi performed an outdoor concert at Exhibition Park, a 5-minute walk from my flat. I decided to show up without a ticket and try to find a spot of grass outside the barriers to sit and listen in for free. a few other groups of people had the same idea. and it worked marvelously well. no one showed up to shoo us away from the temporary walls erected around the concert venue. we could hear the music very clearly. there was also a small hill nearby with a slightly better view over the top of the walls, affording a glimpse of the live feed on Lewis Capaldi's face projected onto one of the big screens suspended from the stage.

by the end of the evening (around 9:30pm), the little hill was relatively packed with people. I honestly think my no-ticket idea was a better idea than buying a ticket for the chance to stand in the hot and sticky crowd below the stage. as a vertically challenged person, I usually struggle to see anything when stuck in a crowd like that.

the music itself was lovely. it also felt a bit magical to be absorbing the music with everyone else standing on the hill. music has such power to unify people and bridge differences.
