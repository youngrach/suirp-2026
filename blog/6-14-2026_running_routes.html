[blog] running routes

14 June 2026

after a couple of weekend long runs, I think I've discovered my staple long run route for the rest of my time here. Jesmond Dene is a lush strip of woodland surrounding a river, about a mile from my flat. this strip of woodland links at least 3 other parks together, providing more than 7 weekends' worth of opportunity for getting lost down various trails. I'm looking forward to acquainting myself with more of the Dene next weekend. and the weekend after that. and the weekend after that one.

this city is overall extremely pedestrian-friendly. sidewalks (or "pavements" in British) galore line basically every street, with many pedestrian-only streets within the city centre, and a lovely wide riverwalk at the quayside. it's a joy to run in Newcastle. the weather is chef's kiss when the wind isn't going crazy, which it has been so far. but it's also a glorious celebration of nature to feel such strong gusts that I get buffeted slightly off-course midstride.

and the flowers! my goodness. there are so many wildflowers, both in the various green spaces around town and poking up through cracks in the roads and pavements. poppies bobbing their red, orange, white, or blue heads in the breeze. and some funkier species that I don't know the names of, but that are gorgeous nonetheless. I know it's probably not so beautiful here year-round, but I intend to soak it in as much as I can while I'm here.

in other news, I've found an almond croissant that may be top of my lifetime almond croissant tier list. it belongs to Pink Lane Bakery. I extend my deep gratitude to Pink Lane Bakery for bringing such a perfect specimen into the world.
